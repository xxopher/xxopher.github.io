Case Study 01 // Model ML Paradigm

Single-Family Office: Multi-Asset Due Diligence Harness

TARGET AUM

$650M Multi-Asset

CYCLE TIME IMPACT

12 Days ➔ <4 Hours

CONVERSION OUTCOME

100% Managing Partner Adoption

1. Problem & Friction

A 3-person investment team faced deal-flow bottlenecks when evaluating direct co-investments and processing quarterly GP reporting across unstructured PDFs, resulting in 35 manual hours per deal and lost syndicate allocations.

2. Engagement Delivery & Governance

  • Value Decomposition: Standardized partner evaluation heuristics into a 4-tier programmatic evaluation matrix.
  • Delivery Governance: Deployed a RAID log tracking VDR formatting edge-cases and non-disclosure compliance.
  • HITL Gateways: Built sign-off checkpoints enabling principals to verify extracted debt ratios before memo compilation.

3. Business Results

Reduced analyst processing hours by 88% per opportunity, tripled co-investment deal flow velocity without expanding headcount, and converted a 60-day POC into a multi-year enterprise platform contract.

Automated Citation Tracking

1. Operational Problem

Enterprise compliance officers and wealth advisory teams routinely lost 3-4 hours per client query manually validating policy revisions across fragmented internal PDFs and regulatory databases, facing high liability risk from outdated citations.

2. Delivery Leadership & Methodology

  • Phase 1 (POC Scope): Delivered a containerized reference engine on static regulatory PDFs within 3 sprints.
  • Phase 2 (Hardening): Integrated CI/CD automated linting and security scanning to meet enterprise deployment standards.
  • User Enablement: Ran calibration sessions with users to establish confidence in cell-level and paragraph-level citations.

3. Business Results

Compressed cross-reference cycle times from hours to seconds while providing fully auditable page/paragraph references, meeting non-functional regulatory compliance guidelines.
