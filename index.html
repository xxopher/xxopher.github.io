Model ML Paradigm // Family Office

Multi-Asset Due Diligence & Investment Committee Memo Harness

Target Portfolio

$650M Multi-Asset

Deal Cycle Time

100% Adoption

The Operational Friction

A 3-person single-family office received 30+ confidential information memorandums (CIMs) and private fund updates monthly. Analysts spent 35 manual hours per deal transcribing tables into Excel models, creating a 2-week backlog that routinely caused the firm to lose syndicate allocations.

Engagement Approach & Governance

Scoped and standardized the firm's unwritten evaluation heuristics into a 4-tier decision rubric. Implemented a delivery RAID log to isolate document-parsing edge cases, and introduced mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) checkpoints so investment principals could verify extracted valuation metrics before compiling final deliverables.

Commercial Impact

Compressed deal screening turnaround by 88%, tripling monthly co-investment review capacity without adding headcount. Converted a 60-day proof-of-concept into a multi-year enterprise platform contract.

Enterprise Regulatory Compliance & Knowledge Retrieval RAG

The Operational Friction

Enterprise wealth advisory and compliance personnel lost 3 to 4 hours per client query manually validating policy revisions across disparate, scanned circulars. Relying on keyword lookups carried legal exposure from missed addendums.

Phased Delivery & Execution

Structured the deployment across three delivery milestones: Phase 1 delivered a containerized baseline parser on static PDF circulars; Phase 2 introduced hybrid search to address alphanumeric clause citations; Phase 3 hardened the system with CI/CD linting and PII sanitization gates.

Business Outcome

Compressed policy verification time from hours to seconds while maintaining direct page/paragraph citations that met internal non-functional compliance standards.
